## Search tips

- Use Arabic keywords on the scholars' sites: e.g. `site:binbaz.org.sa حكم التأمين`, `site:binothaimeen.net حكم الموسيقى`, `site:alifta.gov.sa حكم الأسهم`.
- For English answers, islamqa.info/en and the English section of alifta.gov.sa are the best starting points; then follow their references back to the scholar's Arabic page when possible.
- Search phrasing: start with `حكم` (ruling of) + the topic; or `ما حكم` + topic; for worship details use the act's name (e.g. `سجود السهو`).
- Always open and read the page. Do not cite a search-result snippet as the fatwa.
- Prefer the page that is the scholar's own words (his site, his book) over a summary of him.

## Citing

- Website: Scholar - short title - link to the exact page.
- Book: Scholar - Book title - volume/page (and fatwa number for al-Lajnah).
- islamqa: "islamqa.info, question no. XXXX" + link.
