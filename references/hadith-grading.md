# Reporting hadith and their grades

## Grades (short meaning for the user)

- **Sahih** (صحيح): authentic. Can be used as proof.
- **Hasan** (حسن): good. Can be used as proof.
- **Sahih/Hasan li-ghayrihi**: authentic/good because of supporting chains. Can be used as proof.
- **Da'if** (ضعيف): weak. Not used to establish a ruling.
- **Da'if jiddan** (ضعيف جدا): very weak.
- **Mawdu'** (موضوع): fabricated. Must not be attributed to the Prophet ﷺ except to warn that it is fabricated.
- **La asla lahu** (لا أصل له): no basis, no chain found.

## How to report

Give: the text (or a faithful meaning), the collector and number, and the grade with who graded it.

Examples of the format (not real citations to reuse):
- "Reported by al-Bukhari (no.) and Muslim (no.)" - agreed upon, no further grading needed.
- "Reported by Abu Dawud (no.). Graded sahih by al-Albani in Sahih Abi Dawud."
- "Weak: graded da'if by al-Albani in al-Silsilah al-Da'ifah (no.)."

## Rules

- Hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim are accepted as authentic.
- For anything else, look up the grading (Dorar MCP or dorar.net `/hadith` search) rather than relying on memory. Prefer gradings by al-Albani, Ibn Baz, al-Arna'ut-checked editions as quoted by Dorar, and the classical scholars (Ibn Hajar, al-Dhahabi, al-Nawawi).
- If gradings differ, mention the main ones.
- Never give a hadith number or a grading you have not checked. If you could not check it, say "grading not verified".
- When a popular saying is weak or fabricated, tell the user kindly and, if possible, give an authentic hadith on the same topic instead.
